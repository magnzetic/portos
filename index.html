Nabila Muthia Putri

A writer and author drawn to turning ideas and everyday experiences into stories that feel familiar, yet slightly off-center.

With a foundation in Computer Science, structure and logic quietly guide the creative process, allowing stories to unfold with intention and clarity. Much of the work circles themes of technology, education, and niche cultural references—shaped by having more than 12 hours screen time.

Beyond writing for others, I'm also actively creating content on my own through @mencupir on Instagram, TikTok, Threads, and X.

I mostly talk about films, but you'll also find me sharing my thoughts on music, comics, and other pop culture interests. I don't want to limit myself to one niche just because social media says I should. I'm a person with different interests, and I'd rather be known for who I am.
